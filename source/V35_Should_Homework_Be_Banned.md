## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> MODERATOR: Good afternoon. Today's motion is: "Homework should be banned in secondary schools." Leo will speak for the motion, and Hana against it. Leo, two minutes, please.
>
> LEO: Thank you. I'd like to argue that homework does more harm than good. Many students already spend seven or eight hours at school. Adding two more hours at night means less sleep, less exercise, and less time with family. And homework is unfair: a student with a quiet room and educated parents has a huge advantage over a student who works after school or shares a room with three siblings. Let's keep learning in the classroom, where every student has the same support.
>
> MODERATOR: Thank you, Leo. Hana?
>
> HANA: Thank you. My opponent makes a fair point about fairness, but banning homework is the wrong solution. Research suggests that for older students, a reasonable amount of homework is linked to better learning, especially when it gives practice that there is no time for in class. Some skills, like reading long texts or solving problems alone, need quiet, independent time. So instead of banning homework, let's make it better: shorter, more meaningful, and with a quiet place at school for students who need one.
>
> MODERATOR: A quick rebuttal, Leo?
>
> LEO: That's true for good homework. But most homework isn't designed that carefully. Until it is, less is more.
>
> MODERATOR: Thank you both. Now it's the audience's turn to vote.

## 2회차 · 문장 단위 직독직해

> 한 문장을 한 호흡에 읽는 연습입니다. 1회차에서 끊었던 지점을 의식하되, 멈추지 않고 이어 읽습니다.

**① MODERATOR: Good afternoon. Today's motion is: "Homework should be banned in secondary schools."**

어휘: moderator 사회자 · motion 논제, 동의 · ban 금지하다

**④ LEO: Thank you. I'd like to argue that homework does more harm than good.**
레오: 감사합니다. 저는 숙제가 득보다 실이 크다고 주장하고 싶습니다.
어휘: argue 주장하다 · do harm 해를 끼치다

**⑤ Many students already spend seven or eight hours at school.**
많은 학생이 이미 학교에서 일고여덟 시간을 보냅니다.

**⑥ Adding two more hours at night means less sleep, less exercise, and less time with family.**
밤에 두 시간을 더 하면 잠도, 운동도, 가족과의 시간도 줄어듭니다.

어휘: unfair 불공정한 · advantage 이점 · sibling 형제자매

**⑧ Let's keep learning in the classroom, where every student has the same support.**
모든 학생이 같은 도움을 받을 수 있는 교실 안에서 배움이 이뤄지게 합시다.

## 낭독 포인트

### 1. 토론의 구조

| 순서 | 화자 | 기능 | 핵심 표현 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 사회자 | 논제와 진행 소개 | Today's motion is… / for the motion… against it |
| 2 | 찬성 | 주장 + 근거 2개 + 결론 | I'd like to argue that… / And homework is unfair… / Let's… |
| 3 | 반대 | 상대 인정 + 반론 + 근거 + 대안 | My opponent makes a fair point… but… / Research suggests… / So instead of… |
| 4 | 찬성 | 짧은 반박 | That's true for…, But… Until it is, less is more. |
| 5 | 사회자 | 마무리 | Now it's the audience's turn to vote. |

### 2. 정중한 반박의 표현

| 표현 | 기능 |
|---|---|
| My opponent makes a fair point about…, but… | 상대 주장을 인정하고 반박 (C04의 "That's partly true, but…") |
| That's true for…. But… | 조건을 붙여 일부 인정 |
| instead of…, let's… | 대안 제시 |

### 3. 근거에 대한 주석

하나가 인용한 "나이가 있는 학생에게 적당한 숙제는 학습과 연관된다"는 내용은 숙제 연구를 종합한 메타분석들(예: Cooper, Robinson, & Patall, 2006, *Review of Educational Research*)에서 보고된 경향, 즉 숙제와 성취의 연관이 초등학생보다 중고등학생에게서 더 뚜렷하다는 결과를 토론 말투로 옮긴 것입니다. 연관(linked)이라고 표현한 것에 주목하세요(D08 참조). 토론과 인물은 창작입니다.
