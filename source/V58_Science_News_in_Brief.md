**라디오 뉴스 낭독 팁.** 분당 160–180단어의 빠른 단신입니다. 다만 각 꼭지의 단서 문장(has not yet been peer reviewed, the sample was small, more work is needed)은 속도를 줄이지 않되 분명하게 읽으세요. 좋은 과학 뉴스는 결과만큼 한계도 들리게 전합니다.

## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> And now the science news in brief.
>
> Astronomers have released the most detailed image yet of a planet-forming disk around a young star about four hundred light-years away. The image shows several gaps in the disk that may be clearing paths left by young planets, though the planets themselves are too faint to see directly.
>
> A new study suggests that a short nap after learning may help people remember new vocabulary. Volunteers who slept for twenty minutes recalled about ten percent more words a week later than those who stayed awake. However, the study involved only forty students and has not yet been peer reviewed.
>
> Engineers in the Netherlands have tested a floating solar farm on a reservoir for two years. They report that the water kept the panels slightly cooler, which can improve efficiency, and that evaporation from the reservoir fell beneath the panels. Ecologists say more work is needed to understand the effects on fish and water plants.
>
> And finally, a museum in Edinburgh has discovered that a fossil labeled as a common fish for more than a century actually belongs to a species never described before. The discovery was made by a volunteer who was photographing the collection for an online catalog.
>
> That's the science news. More after the break.

### 문단 2

### 문단 3

## 낭독 포인트

### 1. 꼭지별 구조: 결과 + 단서

| 꼭지 | 결과 | 단서 (한계) |
|---|---|---|
| 천문 | the most detailed image yet… several gaps… that **may be** clearing paths left by young planets | **though** the planets themselves are too faint to see directly |
| 수면 | a short nap… **may help** people remember new vocabulary… about ten percent more words | **However**, the study involved only forty students and **has not yet been peer reviewed** |
| 에너지 | kept the panels slightly cooler… evaporation… fell | Ecologists say **more work is needed** to understand the effects on fish and water plants |
| 화석 | actually belongs to a species never described before | (발견의 경위로 마무리: 자원봉사자의 관찰) |

1권 V24가 연구 하나를 길게 보도했다면, V58은 네 연구를 각각 두세 문장으로 압축합니다. 짧아져도 각 꼭지마다 한계 문장이 빠지지 않는다는 점에 주목하세요.

### 2. 과학 보도의 단서 표현

| 표현 | 의미 |
|---|---|
| suggests / may help | 결과의 강도 조절 |
| has not yet been peer reviewed | 동료 심사 전 (사전 인쇄본) |
| involved only forty students | 작은 표본 |
| more work is needed | 추가 연구 필요 |
| though… too faint to see directly | 직접 관찰이 아닌 추론 |

### 3. 마지막 꼭지의 의미

> The discovery was made by a volunteer who was photographing the collection for an online catalog.

한 세기 동안 잘못 분류된 화석을 찾아낸 것은 전문가의 새 장비가 아니라 기록 작업을 하던 사람의 주의 깊은 눈이었습니다. V31(보는 것과 관찰하는 것), V34·V54(기록부와 데이터를 끝까지 읽기)의 주제가 뉴스 한 꼭지로 다시 나타납니다. 네 꼭지 모두 가상의 연구이며, 장소와 수치는 창작입니다.
