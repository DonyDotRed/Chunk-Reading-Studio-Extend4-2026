## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Raise your hand if you feel a little nervous when a plane takes off. [pause] Now keep your hand up if you feel the same way every time you get into a car. [pause] Almost every hand just went down.
>
> And yet, for each kilometer traveled, flying on a commercial airline is far safer than driving. So why does the plane scare us and the car doesn't?
>
> Psychologists who study risk perception have found that we don't judge risks by their numbers. We judge them by how they feel. Two feelings matter most.
>
> The first is dread. Risks that feel uncontrollable, catastrophic, or that could kill many people at once frighten us far more than risks we feel we control. In the plane, you are a passenger. In the car, you hold the wheel.
>
> The second is the unknown. Risks that are new, invisible, or hard to understand feel more dangerous than familiar ones. This is one reason why radiation worries people so much: you cannot see it, smell it, or feel it.
>
> None of this makes our feelings foolish. Dread and the unknown are telling us something real about what we value: control, fairness, and trust. But if we want to make good decisions, we need both the feeling and the number.
>
> So the next time something scares you, ask yourself one simple question: am I afraid because it is dangerous, or because it is dreadful and unknown? [pause] Thank you.

### 2. 연구를 일상어로 옮기기

| 학술적 표현 | 강연 표현 |
|---|---|
| Risk perception is driven by affective rather than statistical evaluation. | we don't judge risks by their numbers. We judge them by how they feel. |
| The "dread risk" factor (perceived lack of control, catastrophic potential) | Risks that feel uncontrollable, catastrophic, or that could kill many people at once |
| The "unknown risk" factor (novelty, unobservability) | Risks that are new, invisible, or hard to understand |

### 3. 근거에 대한 주석

"공포(dread)"와 "미지(unknown)"의 두 차원은 폴 슬로빅(Paul Slovic) 등의 위험 인식 연구(이른바 심리측정 패러다임)에서 나온 개념으로, 슬로빅의 1987년 《Science》 논문 "Perception of Risk"가 대표 문헌입니다. 이 연구들에서 원자력은 두 차원 모두에서 높게 인식되는 대표 사례로 자주 언급됩니다. 이동 거리당 민간 항공의 안전성이 자동차보다 훨씬 높다는 점은 교통안전 통계에서 일반적으로 보고되는 내용입니다. 강연은 오리지널 창작입니다.
