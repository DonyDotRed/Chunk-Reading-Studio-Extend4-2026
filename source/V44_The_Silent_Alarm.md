## 학습 방법

| 단계 | 할 일 | 권장 횟수 |
|---|---|---|
| 1회차 | 청크 단위 직독직해. 청크마다 끊어 소리 내어 읽고 바로 한국어 뜻을 떠올린다 | 3회 |
| 2회차 | 문장 단위 직독직해. 한 문장을 한 호흡으로 읽고 의미를 확인한다 | 3회 |
| 3회차 | 원문만 보고 처음부터 끝까지 낭독한다. 시간을 재서 기록한다 | 3회 이상 |

낭독 속도는 분당 단어 수(WPM)로 기록합니다. 288단어를 3분에 읽으면 약 96 WPM입니다. 회독이 거듭될수록 속도가 오르고 끊기는 지점이 줄어드는지 확인하세요.

**미스터리 낭독 팁.** 기록부와 화면의 내용(시각, 메시지 상태)은 사실을 읽듯 평탄하고 정확하게, 인물들의 추론("So the alarm did go off.")은 깨달음의 순간처럼 한 박자 쉬고 읽으세요. 마지막 문단은 범인을 밝히는 대신 교훈을 말하므로 차분하게 마무리합니다.

## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> At seven in the morning, the ultra-low freezer in Room 214 was at minus forty degrees instead of minus eighty. Somewhere in the night, the compressor had failed.
>
> "The alarm should have called someone," said Dr. Klein, the lab manager. "It's tested every month. It worked perfectly two weeks ago."
>
> The security guard had heard nothing. The freezer's own beeper was loud, but Room 214 was at the end of a long corridor behind two closed doors.
>
> Kenji, a postdoc who had joined the lab in April, opened the monitoring system on his laptop. The log was clear. At 02:14, the temperature had passed minus seventy. At 02:15, the system had sent an emergency text. Status: delivered.
>
> "So the alarm did go off," Kenji said slowly. "It just didn't reach any of us."
>
> He clicked on the contact list. There was only one name on it: Daniel Moreau.
>
> Dr. Klein closed her eyes. Daniel had run the freezers for six years. He had moved to a new job in another city in March, and his tasks had been divided among three people. The monthly test had been passed to Kenji.
>
> "I test it by pressing the test button," Kenji said. "The screen says 'Message sent,' and I write 'OK' in the logbook." He paused. "I never checked who it was sent to."
>
> Somewhere in another city, Daniel had probably woken up at a quarter past two, read the message, and wondered why his old lab was still calling him.
>
> Nobody had done anything wrong, exactly. The system had worked. The test had passed. The failure lived in the gap between people, in the moment when a job changed hands and one small line of a list did not.

## 낭독 포인트

### 1. 공정한 단서

| 단서 | 위치 | 의미 |
|---|---|---|
| It worked perfectly two weeks ago. | 2문단 | 시스템 자체는 정상 (경보 장치 고장이 아님) |
| a postdoc who had joined the lab in April | 4문단 | 켄지는 다니엘이 떠난 뒤 합류 |
| Status: delivered. | 4문단 | 문자는 분명히 어딘가에 전달됨 |
| He had moved to a new job in another city in March | 7문단 | 연락처가 갱신되지 않았을 가능성 |

V34와 달리 이 이야기에는 "범인"이 없습니다. 원인은 사람이 아니라 업무 인수인계의 틈입니다.

### 2. 사실과 추론의 목소리

| 종류 | 예 | 낭독 |
|---|---|---|
| 기록의 사실 | At 02:14, the temperature had passed minus seventy. At 02:15, the system had sent an emergency text. Status: delivered. | 평탄하고 정확하게, 화면을 읽듯 |
| 추론 | "So the alarm **did** go off… It just didn't reach any of us." | 깨달음, did에 강세 |
| 고백 | "I never checked who it was sent to." | 낮고 천천히 |
| 상상 | Daniel had probably woken up at a quarter past two… | 부드럽게, 약간의 아이러니 |

### 3. 결말의 교훈

> The failure lived in the gap between people, in the moment when a job **changed hands** and one small line of a list **did not**.

"change hands(주인이 바뀌다)"를 앞에서 쓰고 "did not"으로 받아, 바뀐 것과 바뀌지 않은 것을 대조합니다. 1권 V17(복창)과 C04(답변 확인)처럼, 안전은 "확인했다"는 기록이 아니라 "누구에게 닿는지"를 확인하는 데서 나온다는 주제입니다.
