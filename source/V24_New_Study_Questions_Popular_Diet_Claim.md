## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Eating only during an eight-hour window each day does not lead to more weight loss than eating the same number of calories spread across the whole day, according to a new study published this week.
>
> The study, conducted by researchers at the University of Northfield, followed 240 adults with obesity for twelve months. Participants were randomly assigned either to time-restricted eating or to a control group, and both groups were given the same daily calorie target.
>
> After a year, participants in the time-restricted group had lost an average of 5.1 kilograms, compared with 4.6 kilograms in the control group. The difference of half a kilogram was not statistically significant.
>
> "Our results suggest that the timing of meals matters much less than the total amount people eat," said Dr. Hannah Okafor, the study's lead author. "For people who find a fixed eating window easier to follow, it may still be a useful tool. But it is not a shortcut."
>
> The authors cautioned that the study had limitations. Most participants were middle-aged, and food intake was reported by the participants themselves, which may not always be accurate. The study was funded by a national research council, and the authors reported no conflicts of interest.
>
> Dr. Peter Lindgren, a nutrition researcher who was not involved in the study, called the findings "a useful correction" to popular claims that time-restricted eating works regardless of calories. "One study won't settle the question," he added, "but it's a well-designed trial, and it points in a clear direction."
>
> Experts advise that anyone considering a major change in diet, especially people with diabetes or those taking medication, should first speak with a doctor.

## 2회차 · 문장 단위 직독직해

> 한 문장을 한 호흡에 읽는 연습입니다. 1회차에서 끊었던 지점을 의식하되, 멈추지 않고 이어 읽습니다.

### 문단 1

**① Eating only during an eight-hour window each day does not lead to more weight loss than eating the same number of calories spread across the whole day, according to a new study published this week.**
이번 주 발표된 새 연구에 따르면, 하루 8시간 안에만 먹는 것이 같은 칼로리를 하루 종일 나눠 먹는 것보다 체중을 더 줄이지는 않는다.
어휘: window (시간) 창, 시간대 · weight loss 체중 감소 · calorie 칼로리 · spread 퍼뜨리다

### 문단 2

**② The study, conducted by researchers at the University of Northfield, followed 240 adults with obesity for twelve months.**
노스필드 대학교 연구진이 수행한 이 연구는 비만 성인 240명을 12개월 동안 추적했다.
어휘: conduct 수행하다 · follow 추적하다 · obesity 비만

**③ Participants were randomly assigned either to time-restricted eating or to a control group, and both groups were given the same daily calorie target.**
참가자들은 시간 제한 식사군과 대조군에 무작위로 배정되었고, 두 군 모두 같은 하루 칼로리 목표를 받았다.
어휘: randomly 무작위로 · assign 배정하다 · time-restricted eating 시간 제한 식사 · control group 대조군 · target 목표

### 문단 3

**④ After a year, participants in the time-restricted group had lost an average of 5.1 kilograms, compared with 4.6 kilograms in the control group.**
1년 뒤 시간 제한 식사군은 평균 5.1킬로그램, 대조군은 4.6킬로그램을 감량했다.

**⑤ The difference of half a kilogram was not statistically significant.**
0.5킬로그램의 차이는 통계적으로 유의하지 않았다.

### 문단 4

**⑥ "Our results suggest that the timing of meals matters much less than the total amount people eat," said Dr. Hannah Okafor, the study's lead author.**
"우리 결과는 식사 시간이 먹는 총량보다 훨씬 덜 중요하다는 것을 시사합니다." 연구의 제1저자인 해나 오카포 박사의 말이다.
어휘: timing 시점 · lead author 제1저자

**⑦ "For people who find a fixed eating window easier to follow, it may still be a useful tool.**
"정해진 식사 시간대가 지키기 더 쉬운 사람에게는 여전히 유용한 방법일 수 있습니다.

**⑧ But it is not a shortcut."**
하지만 지름길은 아닙니다."
어휘: shortcut 지름길

### 문단 5

**⑨ The authors cautioned that the study had limitations.**
저자들은 이 연구에 한계가 있다고 당부했다.
어휘: caution 경고하다

**⑩ Most participants were middle-aged, and food intake was reported by the participants themselves, which may not always be accurate.**
참가자 대부분이 중년이었고, 식사량은 참가자 본인이 보고한 것이어서 항상 정확하지는 않을 수 있다.
어휘: middle-aged 중년의 · intake 섭취량

**⑪ The study was funded by a national research council, and the authors reported no conflicts of interest.**
이 연구는 국가 연구 위원회의 지원을 받았으며, 저자들은 이해 상충이 없다고 밝혔다.
어휘: fund 자금을 대다 · council 위원회 · conflict of interest 이해 상충

### 문단 6

**⑫ Dr. Peter Lindgren, a nutrition researcher who was not involved in the study, called the findings "a useful correction" to popular claims that time-restricted eating works regardless of calories.**
이 연구에 참여하지 않은 영양학 연구자 피터 린드그렌 박사는, 이번 결과가 시간 제한 식사가 칼로리와 상관없이 효과가 있다는 대중적 주장에 대한 "유용한 바로잡기"라고 평가했다.
어휘: nutrition 영양 · involved 관여한 · correction 바로잡기

**⑬ "One study won't settle the question," he added, "but it's a well-designed trial, and it points in a clear direction."**
"연구 하나로 문제가 결론 나지는 않겠지만, 잘 설계된 시험이고 분명한 방향을 가리킵니다." 그가 덧붙였다.
어휘: settle 결론짓다 · trial (임상) 시험

### 문단 7

**⑭ Experts advise that anyone considering a major change in diet, especially people with diabetes or those taking medication, should first speak with a doctor.**
전문가들은 식단을 크게 바꾸려는 사람, 특히 당뇨병이 있거나 약을 복용하는 사람은 먼저 의사와 상의하라고 권한다.

### 3. 기사에 대한 주석
