## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> When we have a fever, our first instinct is usually to get rid of it. But a fever is not simply something that happens to us when we are ill. It is something the body does on purpose.
>
> Body temperature is controlled by a small region of the brain called the hypothalamus, which works much like the thermostat in a house. Normally, it is set at around 37 degrees Celsius. When the immune system detects an infection, it releases chemical signals that travel to the brain and turn the thermostat up.
>
> This explains a familiar experience. At the start of a fever, you may feel cold and begin to shiver, even though your temperature is already rising. Your body is not confused; it is simply trying to reach its new, higher target. When the infection is under control and the thermostat is turned down again, the opposite happens: you feel hot and start to sweat. This is what people mean when they say a fever "breaks."
>
> Why would the body choose to raise its temperature when it is already under attack? Studies suggest several benefits. Many bacteria and viruses reproduce less efficiently at higher temperatures, and some immune cells appear to work faster when the body is warmer. In experiments with cold-blooded animals such as lizards, which raise their temperature by moving to warmer places when infected, animals that were prevented from doing so were less likely to survive.
>
> None of this means that fever is always helpful. Very high temperatures can be dangerous, and a fever can be exhausting, especially for older people or those with heart disease. Medical advice should be sought for fevers in very young babies, fevers that last more than a few days, or fevers accompanied by confusion, a stiff neck, or difficulty breathing. But for most ordinary infections, a fever is less an enemy than a sign that the body is already fighting back.

### 2. 비유가 설명을 이끄는 방식

온도 조절기 비유 하나로 세 가지 현상이 설명됩니다.

| 현상 | 비유로 본 설명 |
|---|---|
| 열이 남 | 면역 신호가 온도 조절기를 **올림** (turn the thermostat up) |
| 열이 시작될 때 오한 | 실제 온도가 새 설정값보다 낮아 몸이 "춥다"고 판단 |
| 열이 내릴 때 땀 | 설정값이 다시 **내려가** 실제 온도가 "너무 높음" |

좋은 비유는 하나의 그림으로 여러 현상을 설명합니다(U08의 메커니즘 설명과 같은 원리).

### 3. 과학적 근거에 대한 주석

열이 시상하부의 체온 설정값 상승으로 생긴다는 것, 면역 신호(사이토카인과 프로스타글란딘 E2)가 이를 매개한다는 것은 생리학의 표준 설명입니다. 4문단의 도마뱀 실험은 1970년대 Kluger 등의 연구로 널리 알려진 내용이며, 열의 이점에 관한 인간 대상 근거는 상황에 따라 다르므로 본문에서 "Studies suggest", "appear to"로 표현했습니다. 이 글은 교양 해설이며, 개인의 증상에 대한 판단은 의료진과 상담하세요.
