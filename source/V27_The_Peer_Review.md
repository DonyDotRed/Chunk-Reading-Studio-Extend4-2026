## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> [A small office. Late afternoon. NOAH stares at his laptop.]
>
> NOAH: Reviewer 2 hates our paper. Listen to this: "The authors' conclusions are not supported by their data." I can't believe it. Two years of work!
>
> EVA: Let's take a breath. Read me the whole comment, not just the first line.
>
> NOAH: [reading, in a stiff voice] "The authors' conclusions are not supported by their data. In particular, the claim that the method works for all patients is based on a sample of only one hospital. I would encourage the authors to limit their claims or to add an external validation."
>
> EVA: [after a pause] Is that unfair?
>
> NOAH: …No. [long pause] We did write "all patients." In the abstract. Twice.
>
> EVA: Then Reviewer 2 doesn't hate our paper. Reviewer 2 read it carefully, which is more than most people will ever do.
>
> NOAH: [laughing a little] Okay. That's a kinder way to see it. So what do we do?
>
> EVA: Two things. First, we change "all patients" to "patients in this setting." That takes ten minutes. Second, why don't we ask Dr. Moreno whether her hospital could share a small dataset? If it holds up, the paper gets much stronger. If it doesn't, we've learned something before anyone else had to tell us.
>
> NOAH: I'll start the response letter. "We thank the reviewer for this careful reading…"
>
> EVA: [smiling] And this time, mean it.

### 2. 비판을 재해석하는 대화

> NOAH: Reviewer 2 **hates** our paper.
> EVA: Then Reviewer 2 doesn't hate our paper. Reviewer 2 **read it carefully**…

같은 사실(혹독한 심사 의견)을 "공격"에서 "꼼꼼한 읽기"로 바꿔 보는 대화입니다. D15(심사 답변서)의 "We have clarified…"와 C04(비판적 질문에 답하기)의 "That's a fair criticism."이 감정 단계에서는 이렇게 시작됩니다.

### 3. 심사 의견이 실제로 지적한 것

심사위원의 지적은 "단일 병원 표본으로 '모든 환자'를 주장할 수 없다"는 일반화의 문제입니다. 이는 D05(단일 병원 코호트), D11(차이의 설명), D12(한계 진술), U22(성급한 일반화)에서 반복해 다룬 문제이고, 에바의 두 가지 해결책(주장의 범위 축소, 외부 검증 추가)도 Domain Track의 원칙과 그대로 이어집니다.
