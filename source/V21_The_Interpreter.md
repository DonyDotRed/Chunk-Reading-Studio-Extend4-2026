## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> In the booth at the back of the hall, Mira Chen adjusted her headphones and waited for the keynote speaker to begin. For the next forty minutes, three hundred people would hear the speaker's ideas in her voice, and if she did her job well, not one of them would think about her at all.
>
> The speaker was fast, fond of long sentences, and, she soon discovered, fond of jokes. The first two were easy: a story about a lost suitcase, a comment about the weather. The third was a pun, a play on two English words that sound almost the same and mean completely different things.
>
> Three seconds. Maybe four. That was how long she had before the audience in the front rows, the ones who understood English, would start laughing. If the people listening to her laughed too, the room would stay together. If they didn't, half the hall would be laughing at something the other half had never heard.
>
> She could translate the words and lose the joke. She could explain the joke and lose the speaker, who was already into his next sentence. Or she could do what she had been trained never to do and almost always did in moments like this.
>
> "The speaker has just made a joke that is impossible to translate," she said calmly in her own language. "Please laugh now."
>
> Half a second later, the whole hall laughed at the same moment. On the stage, the speaker smiled, delighted, and pressed on, convinced that his pun had worked in every language in the room.
>
> In the booth, Mira took a sip of water and kept going. Nobody would ever know. That, she thought, was the whole point.

## 2회차 · 문장 단위 직독직해

> 한 문장을 한 호흡에 읽는 연습입니다. 1회차에서 끊었던 지점을 의식하되, 멈추지 않고 이어 읽습니다.

### 문단 1

**① In the booth at the back of the hall, Mira Chen adjusted her headphones and waited for the keynote speaker to begin.**
강당 뒤편 통역 부스에서 미라 첸은 헤드폰을 고쳐 쓰고 기조연설자가 시작하기를 기다렸다.
어휘: booth (통역) 부스 · hall 강당 · adjust 조정하다 · keynote speaker 기조연설자

**② For the next forty minutes, three hundred people would hear the speaker's ideas in her voice, and if she did her job well, not one of them would think about her at all.**
앞으로 40분 동안 300명이 그녀의 목소리로 연사의 생각을 듣게 될 것이고, 그녀가 일을 잘 해낸다면 그중 누구도 그녀를 의식하지 않을 것이었다.

### 문단 2

**③ The speaker was fast, fond of long sentences, and, she soon discovered, fond of jokes.**
연사는 말이 빨랐고, 긴 문장을 좋아했으며, 곧 알게 되었지만 농담도 좋아했다.
어휘: be fond of ~을 좋아하다

**④ The first two were easy: a story about a lost suitcase, a comment about the weather.**
처음 두 개는 쉬웠다. 잃어버린 가방 이야기, 날씨에 관한 한마디.

**⑤ The third was a pun, a play on two English words that sound almost the same and mean completely different things.**
세 번째는 말장난이었다. 소리는 거의 같은데 뜻은 완전히 다른 두 영어 단어를 이용한 말놀이였다.

그렇지 않다면 강당의 절반은 나머지 절반이 듣지도 못한 무언가에 웃게 될 것이었다.

## 낭독 포인트

### 1. 자유 간접 화법 (free indirect speech)

인물의 생각을 따옴표 없이 서술 속에 녹이는 기법입니다. 화자의 목소리와 인물의 머릿속 목소리가 겹칩니다.

| 문장 | 누구의 목소리인가 |
|---|---|
| Three seconds. Maybe four. | 미라의 머릿속 계산 (서술처럼 보이지만 생각) |
| She could translate the words and lose the joke. She could explain… Or she could do… | 미라가 떠올리는 선택지 |
| Nobody would ever know. | 미라의 생각 |
| That, she thought, was the whole point. | 서술자가 "she thought"로 생각임을 밝힘 |

낭독할 때는 서술 부분보다 생각 부분을 조금 빠르고 낮게, 혼잣말처럼 읽으면 두 목소리가 구분됩니다.

### 2. 문장 길이의 대비

| 긴 문장 | 짧은 문장 |
|---|---|
| For the next forty minutes, three hundred people would hear the speaker's ideas in her voice, and if she did her job well, not one of them would think about her at all. | Three seconds. Maybe four. |
| On the stage, the speaker smiled, delighted, and pressed on, convinced that his pun had worked in every language in the room. | Nobody would ever know. |

긴 문장은 상황과 생각을 펼치고, 짧은 문장은 긴장과 결론을 찍습니다.

### 3. 가정법으로 그린 머릿속 시나리오

> **If** the people listening to her **laughed** too, the room **would stay** together. **If** they **didn't**, half the hall **would be laughing** at something the other half **had never heard**.

통역사가 몇 초 안에 두 가지 결과를 저울질하는 장면을 가정법 과거로 표현했습니다(U21의 반사실적 사고를 이야기 속 결정의 순간에 쓴 예).

### 4. 이야기에 관한 메모

동시통역사가 번역할 수 없는 농담을 만났을 때 청중에게 "지금 웃어 달라"고 부탁한다는 이야기는 통역사들 사이에서 오래 전해지는 일화로 알려져 있습니다. 이 단편은 그 일화에서 착안한 창작입니다.
