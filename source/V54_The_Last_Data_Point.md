## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> At eleven at night, the p-value appeared on Ana's screen: 0.03. After eighteen months of collecting data from four clinics, the association they had hoped for was finally there: higher fasting glucose, lower scores on the memory test.
>
> She almost sent the result to her supervisor straight away. Instead, out of habit, she made a scatter plot.
>
> Two hundred points sat in a loose cloud, with no obvious pattern. And then, far to the right, alone, was one point with a glucose value of 126 and the lowest memory score in the study.
>
> She removed it and ran the model again. p = 0.41. One patient had been carrying the entire result.
>
> Removing a point because it is inconvenient is not allowed. Ana knew that. The question was not whether the point was annoying but whether it was true.
>
> She traced it back. The patient had been recruited at the fourth clinic, the only one that had joined the study late, after its laboratory had switched to a new reporting system in June. Every other glucose value in her spreadsheet was between four and nine.
>
> Four to nine. Millimoles per liter. And 126…
>
> She did the arithmetic in her head. 126 milligrams per deciliter, divided by eighteen, was seven. An ordinary value, sitting quietly in the middle of the cloud, if it had only been entered in the right units.
>
> The next morning she wrote to her supervisor. The email was not the one she had imagined at eleven the night before: no significant association, one unit conversion error found and corrected, and a suggestion to check every value from the fourth clinic.
>
> Her supervisor replied in two lines. "Disappointing. And exactly right. Better us than Reviewer 2."

## 2회차 · 문장 단위 직독직해

> 한 문장을 한 호흡에 읽는 연습입니다. 1회차에서 끊었던 지점을 의식하되, 멈추지 않고 이어 읽습니다.

### 문단 1

**① At eleven at night, the p-value appeared on Ana's screen: 0.03.**
밤 11시, 아나의 화면에 p값이 떴다. 0.03.
어휘: p-value p값

**② After eighteen months of collecting data from four clinics, the association they had hoped for was finally there: higher fasting glucose, lower scores on the memory test.**
네 곳의 진료소에서 18개월 동안 데이터를 모은 끝에, 그들이 바라던 연관성이 마침내 나타났다. 공복 혈당이 높을수록 기억력 검사 점수가 낮았다.
어휘: clinic 진료소 · fasting glucose 공복 혈당

### 문단 2

**③ She almost sent the result to her supervisor straight away.**
그녀는 결과를 하마터면 곧바로 지도교수에게 보낼 뻔했다.
어휘: straight away 곧바로

**④ Instead, out of habit, she made a scatter plot.**
대신 그녀는 습관처럼 산점도를 그렸다.
어휘: out of habit 습관적으로 · scatter plot 산점도

### 문단 3

**⑤ Two hundred points sat in a loose cloud, with no obvious pattern.**
점 200개가 뚜렷한 패턴 없이 느슨한 구름처럼 흩어져 있었다.
어휘: loose 느슨한 · obvious 뚜렷한

**⑥ And then, far to the right, alone, was one point with a glucose value of 126 and the lowest memory score in the study.**
그리고 저 오른쪽 끝에, 혈당 126에 연구 전체에서 가장 낮은 기억력 점수를 가진 점 하나가 홀로 떨어져 있었다.

### 문단 4

**⑦ She removed it and ran the model again.**
그녀는 그 점을 빼고 모델을 다시 돌렸다.
어휘: remove 제거하다 · run a model 모델을 돌리다

**⑧ p = 0.41.**
p는 0.41이었다.

**⑨ One patient had been carrying the entire result.**
환자 한 명이 결과 전체를 떠받치고 있었던 것이다.
어휘: entire 전체의

### 문단 5

**⑩ Removing a point because it is inconvenient is not allowed.**
불편하다는 이유로 데이터를 빼는 것은 허용되지 않는다.
어휘: inconvenient 불편한

**⑪ Ana knew that.**
아나도 그걸 알았다.

**⑫ The question was not whether the point was annoying but whether it was true.**
문제는 그 점이 성가신지가 아니라, 그것이 사실인지였다.
어휘: annoying 성가신

### 문단 6

**⑬ She traced it back.**
그녀는 그 점의 이력을 거슬러 올라갔다.
어휘: trace back 거슬러 추적하다

**⑭ The patient had been recruited at the fourth clinic, the only one that had joined the study late, after its laboratory had switched to a new reporting system in June.**
그 환자는 네 번째 진료소에서 모집되었는데, 그곳은 6월에 검사실이 새 보고 시스템으로 바뀐 뒤 늦게 연구에 합류한 유일한 진료소였다.
어휘: recruit 모집하다 · switch 바꾸다

**⑮ Every other glucose value in her spreadsheet was between four and nine.**
그녀의 스프레드시트에 있는 다른 혈당 값은 모두 4에서 9 사이였다.

4에서 9. 단위는 mmol/L.

**⑰ And 126…**

**⑱ She did the arithmetic in her head.**
그녀는 머릿속으로 계산했다.

126 mg/dL을 18로 나누면 7이었다.

**⑳ An ordinary value, sitting quietly in the middle of the cloud, if it had only been entered in the right units.**

**㉔ "Disappointing. And exactly right.**

### 3. 단위에 관한 주석

혈당은 나라와 기관에 따라 mmol/L(리터당 밀리몰)과 mg/dL(데시리터당 밀리그램)로 보고되며, 포도당의 경우 mg/dL ÷ 약 18 = mmol/L입니다. 126 mg/dL은 약 7.0 mmol/L로, 공복 혈당 당뇨병 진단 기준값으로 자주 쓰이는 수치이기도 합니다. 다기관 자료를 합칠 때 단위가 섞이는 것은 실제로 흔한 오류입니다. 인물과 연구는 창작입니다.
