## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Mina had exactly four minutes. The platform clock in Munich said 21:56, and the last train to Salzburg left at 22:00 from platform 11. She was standing at platform 5, holding a suitcase with one broken wheel.
>
> She ran. The suitcase bounced and scraped behind her like an angry dog. When she reached platform 11, the doors were still open, and a conductor with a gray mustache was looking at his watch.
>
> "Salzburg?" she asked, out of breath. "Salzburg," he said. "You have twenty seconds. Do you have a ticket?"
>
> "On my phone." She held it up. The screen was black. The battery had died somewhere between the conference dinner and the station.
>
> The conductor raised an eyebrow. Behind him, the doors began to beep. "Get on," he said finally. "We'll sort it out inside."
>
> The train was almost empty. Mina sat down by the window and tried to breathe normally. Outside, the lights of the city slid away and the darkness of the countryside took their place.
>
> Ten minutes later, the conductor came back. "Now," he said, "about that ticket."
>
> "I'm so sorry," Mina said. "My phone is dead. I bought the ticket this morning, I promise. Is there anywhere I can charge it?"
>
> He thought for a moment. Then he took a small cable out of his pocket and plugged it into a socket under the table. "Five minutes," he said. "I'll come back."
>
> When he returned, the phone had woken up. Mina found the ticket and showed it to him. He scanned it, nodded, and pulled the cable out again.
>
> "Do you always carry a charger?" she asked.
>
> "For thirty years I have worked on night trains," he said. "Every night, someone's phone is dead. Every night, someone runs." He smiled under his mustache. "Tonight it was you."
>
> As the train crossed the border, Mina wrote in her notebook: Charge your phone. Fix the suitcase. And next time, thank the conductor by name.
>
> She looked up. His name tag said Herbert. "Thank you, Herbert," she said. "Gute Reise," he answered, and walked on.

"오늘 밤은 당신이었네요."

### 문단 13

**㊳ As the train crossed the border, Mina wrote in her notebook: Charge your phone. Fix the suitcase. And next time, thank the conductor by name.**
기차가 국경을 넘을 때 미나는 공책에 적었다. 휴대폰 충전할 것. 가방 고칠 것. 그리고 다음엔 차장에게 이름을 불러 고맙다고 할 것.
어휘: border 국경

### 문단 14

**㊴ She looked up.**
그녀가 고개를 들었다.

**㊵ His name tag said Herbert.**
그의 이름표에는 헤르베르트라고 적혀 있었다.
어휘: name tag 이름표

**㊶ "Thank you, Herbert," she said.**
"고마워요, 헤르베르트." 그녀가 말했다.

**㊷ "Gute Reise," he answered, and walked on.**
"좋은 여행 되세요." 그는 독일어로 답하고 다시 걸어갔다.
어휘: Gute Reise (독일어) 좋은 여행 되세요 · walk on 계속 걸어가다
